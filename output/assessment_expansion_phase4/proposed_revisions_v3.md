## 2. Revision Protocol & Schema

Every entry in the revision catalog adheres strictly to the required review schema:
- **Asset ID:** Unique asset identifier from the Phase 4.0 expansion manifest.
- **Platform & Format:** Channel target and content format specification.
- **Target Field:** Exact metadata or content field proposed for modification.
- **Complete Original Wording:** Unabridged, full original text from the asset draft.
- **Complete Proposed Wording:** Unabridged, complete replacement text with zero trailing ellipses or incomplete thoughts.
- **Reason for Change:** Detailed editorial, compliance, and technical rationale justifying the modification.
- **Whether Factual Verification is Still Required:** Explicit determination of whether the proposed text introduces verifiable empirical claims or remains grounded editorial guidance.
