## 1. Executive Summary & Review Audit Corrections

Following the Phase 4.1 editorial audit of `proposed_revisions.md`, four systematic deficiencies were identified and are corrected in this Version 2 proposal:

1. **YouTube Shorts Hooks (Category 1 — 7 Assets):**
   - *Problem in v1:* Truncated 45-character metadata strings were replaced with a single generic phrase (`"[0-3s Kinetic Card] Stop overcomplicating your workflow."`) duplicated across all seven assets.
   - *Correction in v2:* Each of the seven assets now receives a fully written, complete, topic-specific opening hook that eliminates the repetitive phrase while faithfully preserving the core subject matter of the source segment.

2. **X Thread Endings (Category 2 — 6 Assets):**
   - *Problem in v1:* Concluding takeaways followed a rigid and repetitive boilerplate formula (`"3/3 Tactical Takeaway: In your next production sprint on '<Topic>', prioritize source-to-draft traceability before scaling output volume."`).
   - *Correction in v2:* Each thread now concludes with a distinct, actionable, platform-native takeaway tailored directly to the specific topic and workflow challenge discussed, without using the repetitive sprint formula.

3. **Audience Retention Claims (Category 3 — 4 Assets in Segment 03):**
   - *Problem in v1:* Text contained unsupported, precise claims asserting that mobile audiences make retention decisions within "milliseconds" or "the opening seconds," accompanied by trailing ellipses.
   - *Correction in v2:* All behavioral assertions are reframed strictly as practical content-design methodology (e.g., structuring opening video sequences to eliminate polite pleasantries in favor of immediate problem statements). Zero pseudo-scientific claims or invented research citations are introduced.

4. **Attribution & Conversion Claims (Category 4 — 6 Assets in Segments 09 & 10):**
   - *Problem in v1:* Included trailing ellipses, incomplete sentences, and exaggerated claims linking UTM tagging directly to "pipeline velocity" and revenue acquisition engines.
   - *Correction in v2:* Programmatic UTM tagging is accurately defined as an attribution tracking methodology for identifying campaign referral pathways and session engagement. All proposals are presented as complete, grammatically finished sentences without ellipses. Furthermore, where existing source sentences are already technically accurate, they are explicitly recommended for retention rather than unnecessary modification.
